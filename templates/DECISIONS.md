# Decisions

> Why the project is the way it is. One entry per decision that would be expensive to revisit.
>
> This is the file you will thank yourself for. Three months from now someone — possibly you —
> will ask "why is it built like this?", and the answer needs to be one search away, not an
> archaeological dig through a dated log.
>
> Append new decisions at the top. Do not delete superseded ones: mark them and link forward.
> A decision you reversed is still information, and the reason you reversed it is often the
> most valuable line in the file.
